# Limitations

1. **Oracle masks:** Ground-truth masks are used as detections. Segmentation quality, detector recall, object discovery, and detector identity errors are outside the evaluation.
2. **Frozen representation:** DINOv2 ViT-S/14 is frozen. This project does not train or claim to improve a visual representation.
3. **Simple confidence model:** The confidence estimator is unweighted standardized logistic regression by default. It may not represent complex interactions or remain calibrated under distribution shift; held-out Brier score and ECE are reported, and optional class weighting changes the fitted class prior.
4. **Single dataset:** DAVIS 2017 alone cannot establish generalization to other video domains, camera conditions, object categories, or annotation styles.
5. **Rejection/assignment-conflict trade-off:** Confidence rejection and global one-to-one assignment conflicts are separate outcomes. Either can leave a detection unmatched, start a new predicted identity, and increase fragmentation; only confidence rejection indicates that no pair met the threshold.
6. **Precision is incomplete:** Higher association precision alone does not imply better identity continuity or tracking. Recall, coverage, identity switches, fragmentation, and counts must be considered together.
7. **Calibration is separate:** Brier score, ECE, and reliability diagrams assess candidate probabilities. They are not substitutes for tracking metrics, and logistic regression probabilities are not assumed calibrated.
8. **No independent test split:** The current loader uses DAVIS TrainVal's official training and validation lists and does not define an independent labeled test split. Calibration uses training sequences only; validation is held out from fitting, and threshold curves are diagnostic. Results are validation results rather than an untouched test estimate.
9. **Natural and artificial absence:** This version analyzes natural object absence only. Artificial observation removal is not implemented and must not be mixed with natural-occlusion measurements if added.
10. **Novelty:** A successful experiment does not establish publication-level novelty. The contribution is an empirical investigation, not a claim to be the first method using memory, uncertainty, or temporal association.

Additional practical limitations include a remaining train/inference memory-state mismatch. Training candidates replay thresholded cosine decisions, while inference accepts based on learned probabilities plus geometric features. A different decision changes identity ownership, appearance prototypes, memory ages, and therefore future candidate features; this can materially affect long-sequence probability estimates. No iterative feedback or convergence claim is made. Other limitations are sensitivity to mask quality despite oracle annotations, finite DAVIS sequence counts, and the fact that the simplified last-box motion feature does not extrapolate velocity. IDF1 and HOTA are omitted until a validated implementation and protocol are added.
